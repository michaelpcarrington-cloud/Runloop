Long Run Progression

Three rules that matter more than the numbers

1
Long runs go slow. 6:45–7:15/km. You're building endurance, not racing. Goal pace lives in Thursday's run.
2
Niggle = repeat, don't push. If something hurts, redo last week instead of progressing. Seven months means you can afford patience.
3
Step-back weeks are non-negotiable. The lighter weeks let your body absorb the load. Don't skip them because you feel good.
